// 04 — SOLVED FOR YOU

The hardest part of co-founding, handled.

"How do we split this fairly?" kills more collaborations than bad ideas do. GRUPO ships every build with a default model so nobody has to improvise a cap table over DMs.

Revenue Share

Best for: pre-revenue ideas, short sprints

Everyone takes a % of net revenue for a fixed window (e.g. 12–24 months). No valuation needed, no risk of diluting an idea that hasn't earned a cent yet.

Milestone Equity

Best for: long-term ventures, co-founders

Ownership % vests as agreed milestones ship — not just as time passes. Protects the founder from a co-builder who disappears after week two.

GRUPO RECOMMENDS

Hybrid Vesting "Split Sheet"

Best for: almost everything on the platform

Contributors earn revenue share now, which converts into real equity once they cross a milestone threshold — like a SAFE, but for collaboration instead of capital.

Platform fee only on realized revenue

GRUPO takes a flat 5% (3% on Founder plan) of revenue actually processed through the platform — never a cut of unrealized equity. We only get paid when builders get paid.

Reputation replaces enforcement

Every split, deadline, and dispute affects a builder's public Builder Score. Bad actors can't just create a new account and re-enter the marketplace.

Escrow on first payout

First revenue event on any build is held in escrow for 5 days so every co-builder can confirm the split matches the signed agreement before funds release.
